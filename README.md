<h2>Description</h2>
	<p>Kingless is a 2D roguelike game with RPG elements that tries to differentiate itself from other roguelikes by having an interesting story that ties into the meta progresssion.
The game is about a king that one day wakes up in the graveyard of a village in his kingdom, in the form of a skeleton. Not knowing what happened to him and his kingdom, and being unable to speak, he depends on the information offered to him by a mysterious stranger. The king has to battle through a series of caves and catacombs to reach his his castle and face the evil that cursed him.
	</p>
  
  <img src="https://imgur.com/mGnqoOJ.png"></img>
<h2>Gameplay</h2> 
<p>There are two main gameplay areas: the village and the dungeon. 
	In the village, the player can interact with 4 different NPCs which have progressive dialogue based on the player's achievements and gameplay experience (number of deaths, current player level, etc.). From one of the NPCs, the player can purchase helpful upgrades such as a map of the dungeon. The Mysterious stranger is an important character with which the player can trade _souls_, found in the dungeon, for story progression and information. The player can also increase their character level in the village, becoming more powerful.

In the dungeon the palyer has to battle through 3 different randomly generated levels in order to reach the final boss fight. Everytime the player dies, the next trip through the dungeons will be a different experiences due to the generated nature of the levels.
</p>
